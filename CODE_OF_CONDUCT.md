# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully, discuss the work rather than the person, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, or disclosure of another person's private information.

Technical disagreement is expected. Keep claims specific, provide evidence where possible, and make room for correction. Project spaces may remove content or restrict participation when conduct makes collaboration unsafe or materially disrupts the project.

Security reports and personal user situations do not belong in public discussions. Use the private reporting path described in `SECURITY.md`.
