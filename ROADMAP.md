# Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not a delivery promise.

## P0: Reproducible Public Preview

- Fix the clean production frontend build and Sandpack dependency resolution.
- Document one reproducible local and one reproducible container setup.
- Replace preview-only local accounts with production-ready identity and recovery boundaries.
- Add persistent, auditable quota accounting without enabling real payments.
- Run a staged-file credential scan and dependency/security checks in CI.
- Add accessibility and mobile regression coverage for the primary chat path.

## P1: Reliable Opportunity Research

- Expand the source registry one country and one topic at a time.
- Add source-change monitoring, review queues, and stale-answer invalidation.
- Add job-source connectors with explicit cost, rate-limit, provenance, and licensing metadata.
- Complete PDF resume parsing and opt-in voice transcription.
- Add source-backed job matching without automated applications or employer outreach.

## P2: Sustainable Access

- Validate free, standard, advanced, and metered extended-use economics.
- Add a payment provider only after account, ledger, refund, tax, and support requirements are reviewed.
- Separate subscription entitlement from high-cost third-party retrieval credits.
- Add user-controlled export and deletion for confirmed profile data.

## Explicitly Out of Scope for the Current Preview

- Automatically submitting job or visa applications.
- Presenting the service as a licensed lawyer or immigration adviser.
- Guaranteeing employment, visa approval, policy completeness, or uninterrupted availability.
- Crawling an entire government or commercial website without a scoped research need and rights review.
